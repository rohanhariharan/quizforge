QuizForge

Turn any document into a timed quiz.

Upload a PDF, Word file, slide deck, image, or text file. QuizForge writes detailed questions with LaTeX, graphs, and worked solutions, then runs them as a 10-question quiz (20 minutes) or a 20-question test (40 minutes) — with MathQuill answers, a timer, and auto-submit.
